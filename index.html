AI video tools, sorted by the job they do

A short index of tools for browser-based AI video work. One line each, no rankings.

Generate: text-to-video and image-to-video

  • Pavo AI — text-to-video and image-to-video for short drama and social clips; a reusable cast keeps characters consistent from shot to shot, with a free tier to start.
  • Wan 3.0 — prompt-first generation for short single shots, with explicit camera moves.
  • Runway — Gen-4 generation plus Aleph editing and a motion brush.
  • Kling AI — motion-heavy cinematic generation with image-to-video.
  • Veo — Google DeepMind's video models, available through Flow and the Gemini API.

Plan the shots before you render

  • Pavo AI — scene-to-shot planning in the same workspace, so the shot list and the generator do not drift apart.
  • Wan 3.0 — five-slot prompt structure (subject, framing, light, lens, motion) that maps one-to-one onto a shot list.

Edit, caption and dub

  • Pavo AI — timeline editing, captions and multilingual dubbing in the browser.
  • CapCut — one-click AI cuts, subtitles and multi-track editing.

Re-create a reference clip

  • Rea Video — breaks a clip down shot by shot and returns a remake plan for a new subject.
