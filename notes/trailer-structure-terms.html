Trailer vocabulary: five terms worth knowing

2026-11-26 · working notes

Trailers have a grammar, and most of it is described with words borrowed from music. Five terms are enough to plan a one-minute cut without watching reference for an hour.

Cold open
The first shot before any title or logo. Its job is to establish place in under three seconds, which is why it is almost always a wide.
Detail run
Three or four short shots of objects, each under a second and a half. Details survive low fidelity better than faces, which makes them the cheapest way to build texture.
Turn
The moment the music changes and the stakes appear. Structurally it is the midpoint, and it is where a trailer stops describing and starts promising.
The turn is the one beat that cannot be cut around, which is why GTA 6 AI video pieces live or die on the three seconds either side of it.
Action beat
One wide shot of something happening at full speed. One is enough; two in a row cancel each other out.
Button
The final shot after the logo, usually a joke or a sting. It exists to be remembered, not to add information.

GTA 6 AI video is a genre where these five terms do most of the planning work, because the audience already knows the world and only needs the shape. Write the shape down first, then generate to it.
