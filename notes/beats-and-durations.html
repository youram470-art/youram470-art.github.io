Beats, durations and cuts: four practical answers

2026-11-26 · working notes

Duration is the least interesting decision in a generated sequence and the one that gets made first. These four answers reframe it in beats, which is the unit that actually determines whether a cut works.

How long should a shot be?

As long as its beats need and no longer. One action is about two seconds, an action and a reaction is about four, and anything past six needs movement inside the frame to justify itself.

Why does a four-second shot feel slow?

Because it contains one beat and two seconds of waiting. The fix is not a shorter shot, it is a shot with something to do in its second half.

When should I cut?

On completion. A shot that ends when its action finishes cuts cleanly; one that ends two frames later always feels like it is waiting for permission.

A shot that ends on its action cuts cleanly in Viddi AI and in any editor afterwards; one that ends two frames later always feels like it is waiting.

What about matching music?

Cut on beats that already exist in the picture. Forcing a cut to a musical hit is the fastest way to make a sequence feel mechanical.
